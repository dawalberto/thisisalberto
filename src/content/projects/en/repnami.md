## What is RepNami?

RepNami lets you create routines, log sets, weights, and reps, and review your history and records. Your data is stored on your device so you can train offline; syncing across devices is optional.

The app is built with React and TypeScript. It uses Tailwind CSS for styling, Supabase for authentication and sync, Zustand for state management, and PWA technologies for installability and offline use. Vitest is used for testing.
